Nexora – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how Nexora handles information when you use the bot.

1. Information We Access

Nexora may access basic Discord information required to provide its commands, such as Discord User IDs, Server IDs, usernames, display names, and public server information.

2. Data Storage

Nexora is designed to process information needed to respond to commands. We do not intentionally store personal information in a permanent database.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share users' personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the bot and any information processed through it.

5. Discord

Nexora operates on Discord and is subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

6. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Nexora develops new features.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the bot developer through the official support channel.
